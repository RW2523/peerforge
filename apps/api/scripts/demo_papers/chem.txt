Photocatalytic Degradation of Microplastics in Freshwater

Section 1. Introduction
A TiO2-based photocatalyst is evaluated for polyethylene microplastic degradation.

Section 2. Methods
2.1 Materials. Commercial TiO2 nanoparticles; batch-to-batch variation not characterised.
2.2 Conditions. Deionised water under UV-C at 25 °C. Natural organic matter, present in any real freshwater, is absent from all runs.

Section 3. Results
Table 1 reports 34% mass loss over 72 hours. Figure 2 shows FTIR spectra before and after irradiation.

Section 4. Discussion
The authors conclude the effect is robust and recommend adoption in practice.
