Peer Instruction in Large First-Year Physics Cohorts

Section 1. Introduction
Peer instruction is compared with lecture-only delivery across two cohorts of 400 students.

Section 2. Methods
2.1 Design. Non-randomised: cohort 2023 received peer instruction, cohort 2022 did not. Instructor differed between cohorts.
2.2 Outcome. Final exam mark. No pre-test; prior attainment not controlled.

Section 3. Results
Table 1 shows a 6.1-point higher mean in the peer-instruction cohort (p < 0.001). Figure 1 plots the mark distribution.

Section 4. Discussion
The authors conclude the effect is robust and recommend adoption in practice.
