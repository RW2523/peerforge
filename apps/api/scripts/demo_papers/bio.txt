CRISPR Off-Target Effects in Primary Human Hepatocytes

Section 1. Introduction
Genome-wide off-target profiling of a therapeutic guide RNA in primary hepatocytes from four donors.

Section 2. Methods
2.1 Cell Sourcing. Primary hepatocytes from four deceased donors; donor age, sex and disease history not reported.
2.2 Off-Target Detection. GUIDE-seq performed at a single timepoint (72h). No orthogonal validation by targeted amplicon sequencing.

Section 3. Results
Table 1 lists 14 candidate off-target sites. Editing efficiency at the on-target locus was 68% (SD 11). One intergenic site showed 3.2% indels.

Section 4. Discussion
The authors conclude the effect is robust and recommend adoption in practice.
