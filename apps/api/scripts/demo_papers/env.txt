Urban Tree Canopy and Summer Surface Temperature

Section 1. Introduction
Satellite-derived canopy cover is regressed on land surface temperature across 32 cities.

Section 2. Methods
2.1 Data. Landsat 8 thermal bands, single cloud-free scene per city, all from July 2021.
2.2 Analysis. OLS with city fixed effects. Spatial autocorrelation not addressed; no control for surface albedo or building density.

Section 3. Results
Table 2 reports -0.11 °C per percentage point of canopy (SE 0.02). Figure 4 maps residuals for three cities.

Section 4. Discussion
The authors conclude the effect is robust and recommend adoption in practice.
