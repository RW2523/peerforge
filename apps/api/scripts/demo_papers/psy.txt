Smartphone Mindfulness and Undergraduate Exam Anxiety

Section 1. Introduction
A six-week app-based mindfulness programme is evaluated against a waitlist for exam anxiety.

Section 2. Methods
2.1 Participant Selection. Forty undergraduates recruited by flyer from one institution. Allocation by coin flip performed by the first author, who also delivered the intervention. No active control; comparison arm was a waitlist. Not preregistered.
2.2 Measures. Self-report anxiety scale at baseline and week six. Test-retest reliability not assessed; outcome assessment unblinded.

Section 3. Results
Table 1 reports group means. Intervention fell 12.4 points versus 2.1 in waitlist (d = 1.31, p = 0.004). Two waitlist participants withdrew and were excluded.

Section 4. Discussion
The authors conclude the effect is robust and recommend adoption in practice.
