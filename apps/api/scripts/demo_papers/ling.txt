Automatic Detection of Hedging in Academic Writing

Section 1. Introduction
A transformer classifier is trained to detect hedging devices across four disciplines.

Section 2. Methods
2.1 Corpus. 2,000 sentences annotated by one rater; no second rater, so agreement is unmeasured.
2.2 Splits. Random sentence-level split, so sentences from the same paper appear in both train and test.

Section 3. Results
Table 1 reports F1 of 0.89 overall. Table 2 breaks results down by discipline; philosophy is lowest at 0.71.

Section 4. Discussion
The authors conclude the effect is robust and recommend adoption in practice.
