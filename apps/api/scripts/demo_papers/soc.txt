Remote Work and Reported Loneliness Among Early-Career Staff

Section 1. Introduction
Survey of 610 early-career employees relating remote-work days to a loneliness scale.

Section 2. Methods
2.1 Sampling. Convenience sample recruited through two professional networks; response rate 14%.
2.2 Measures. Single-item loneliness question rather than a validated scale. Cross-sectional; causal language used throughout the discussion.

Section 3. Results
Table 1 reports r = 0.28 between remote days and loneliness. Table 3 stratifies by living arrangement.

Section 4. Discussion
The authors conclude the effect is robust and recommend adoption in practice.
